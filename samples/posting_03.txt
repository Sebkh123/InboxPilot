
Lundbeck is on a clear journey to become a bionic company—where data, AI, and digital capabilities are embedded in how we make decisions, scale responsibly, and create value.

Enterprise Data plays a strategic enablement role in this journey. We do not own business outcomes—we enable them by building strong, secure, and scalable data foundations that power analytics, BI, and AI across the organization.

We are looking for a Data Platform Engineer to help develop and evolve our SAP data and analytics platform landscape, with a focus on SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), SAP Datasphere, and the future adoption of SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC).

You do not need to be an expert in all these technologies from day one. We are equally interested in someone with experience in SAP technologies and an interest in platform engineering, or someone with a strong engineering background who is motivated to develop expertise within the SAP ecosystem.

What matters most is your technical curiosity, engineering mindset, and ambition to build reliable, automated, and scalable enterprise platforms.

Your new role 

You will focus on hands-on platform engineering: configuration, automation, deployments, integration, security, and lifecycle management. You will work closely with Platform Architects, Solution Architects, and Platform Engineers across our other enterprise data platforms.

Your responsibilities will include:

Engineer and improve SAC, SAC Planning, and Datasphere capabilities, and help enable BDC as our landscape evolves. 
Strengthen automation, CI/CD, deployment practices, and reusable engineering standards using appropriate SAP tools and APIs. 
Support technical configuration, access controls, security, testing, changes, releases, and platform lifecycle management. 
Collaborate across SAP, Snowflake, and Microsoft platforms on integration patterns and common engineering practices. 
Work with our Application Management Services (AMS) partner on complex technical issues, root cause analysis, change implementation, knowledge transfer, and operational handover. 
Explore emerging platform capabilities and contribute to continuous technical improvement. 


Your future team 

You will join the Enterprise Data Platform team within Enterprise Digital Enablement, responsible for the technical foundations of Lundbeck’s enterprise data platforms.

We work across SAP, Snowflake, dbt, Microsoft Fabric, sharing expertise and standards across technologies. Our AMS partners handle day-to-day operational monitoring and support, while our engineers focus on evolving the platforms and improving their capabilities.

The position is based in Valby, Copenhagen, Denmark.

What You Bring To The Team

You are curious, collaborative, and motivated by making technology more reliable, automated, and scalable. You might be early in your career or an experienced specialist. We value your engineering potential as well as your existing technical experience.

We would particularly value:

Experience with SAP technology, ideally SAC, Datasphere, SAP BTP, or related cloud services. 
Understanding of platform configuration, administration, integration, or technical operations. 
Interest or experience in automation, APIs, Git, CI/CD, Python, SQL, Terraform, or similar tools. 
A structured approach to security, documentation, troubleshooting, and technical quality. 
The ability to work effectively with architects, engineers, and external service partners. 
SAC experience is a strong starting point, but experience with all the technologies is not required. If you bring relevant skills and the motivation to learn, we encourage you to apply.
