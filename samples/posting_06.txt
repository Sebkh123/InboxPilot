latpay has become one of the fastest-growing payment companies by helping merchants accept payments in-store and online. With 100,000+ merchants relying on us today, every card tap, refund, chargeback and payout has to be captured, reconciled and paid out correctly — every day, across multiple payment providers and markets.

Transactions is one of Flatpay's most critical engineering domains. We own the platform that ingests transaction and settlement data from our payment providers, reconciles it, and turns it into the payouts, settlements and analytics that merchants and the rest of Flatpay depend on.

As a Software Engineer, you'll help build and evolve Flatpay's transaction platform — from how we model payments, settlements and disputes, to how we build event-driven systems that process millions of transactions reliably and connect with the payment providers on the other end. This is an opportunity to join a company that has grown from startup to unicorn at record speed. You'll have real ownership, room to grow, and a direct impact on the money flows that 100,000+ businesses rely on. As we expand across markets and providers, you'll help Flatpay scale its payment data infrastructure.

We're an engineering team that embraces AI tooling. We use AI coding agents as part of how we design, build, test and review software, and we invest in the tooling, guardrails and conventions that make that work well. You don't need to be an expert — curiosity and a willingness to learn and shape how we work with AI are what matter.

If you care about the correctness and reliability that money movement demands, this is a domain where your work will play a central role in Flatpay's future growth.
