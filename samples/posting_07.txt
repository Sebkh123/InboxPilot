bout the job
Part-time full-stack developer for Terminal, the web app used to configure and update WALLHACK devices like our K-001 keyboard and M-001 Mouse.

Responsibilities

Features: build features end to end, across the interface, device communication and backend, such as key remapping, LED effects and online firmware updating.
Backend and infrastructure: maintain the repo, CI/CD, hosting, backend services and data, and the offline (desktop) build, including app signing for macOS and Windows.
UI/UX design: design new screens, flows and interactions, and make complex device settings feel simple. Keep the look and feel consistent with the WALLHACK brand.
Firmware coordination: work closely with our firmware engineers on SDKs, HID protocols and release timing.
Firmware rollouts: integrate each new firmware into Terminal, test it in a sandbox with QC, and push it live.
New SKUs and products: add new products and variants to Terminal, including device detection, layout UI and the firmware update pipeline.
Bugs and polish: fix what QC and the community find, and keep the app fast and clean.
Planning: estimate and sequence the work with the product owner, and flag what is possible when new products are being defined.

Skills

A full-stack developer who can show products you have built end to end, from interface to backend.
A strong design eye: layout, typography, interaction and a feel for what makes software for enthusiasts satisfying to use. Comfortable in Figma or similar.
Solid in TypeScript, a modern frontend framework (React or similar) and backend development (Node.js or similar).
Comfortable running the whole delivery pipeline: repo, CI/CD, hosting and releases.
Willing to get into how the browser talks to hardware (WebHID) and to dig in with our firmware engineers when the SDK is not behaving.
Experience using AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot or similar) as a normal part of your workflow.
Pragmatic and self-directed. You are the only developer on Terminal, working from priorities set by the product owner.
Fluent in English. Danish is not required.
